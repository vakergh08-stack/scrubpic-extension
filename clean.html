Clean photos before you share them

Removes EXIF (camera, date, GPS location), XMP and C2PA / Content Credentials from JPEG, PNG and WebP. Everything happens on this computer, offline. The picture itself is not re-encoded, so quality stays exactly the same.

    Removing metadata does not remove visible watermarks, and platforms may still detect AI content in other ways. For HEIC photos, videos and a full metadata viewer, use scrubpic.com.
